# Dataset attribution

The 200 human-reference training reviews and 200 held-out reviews are derived from Abdessamad Benlahbib, [1000 Movie Reviews (Review + Attached rating + Sentiment polarity) for Reputation Generation](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/38j8b6s2mx/1), Mendeley Data, version 1 (2019), DOI [10.17632/38j8b6s2mx.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/38j8b6s2mx.1). The Mendeley record lists the dataset under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The included files are an adapted subset and split of that collection, combined in the training file with 400 AI-generated movie reviews. The collection says its reviews were extracted from IMDb and was published in 2019, before [ChatGPT launched publicly](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt/). We therefore treat those reviews as human-written references, while noting that the collection does not verify the author of each individual review. Detector flags on source reviews are not proof of AI authorship.

The experiment scripts are provided without a separate open-source license. The Mendeley source material retains its stated license and attribution requirements.
